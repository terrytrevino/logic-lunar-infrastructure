Drive the rover around rocks

Open this course to see the rocks in the cameras above. Replay shows the same starting rocks as static obstacles. Blade and arm actions are available in the driving course.

Use the buttons or arrow keys with focus on a driving control. Lower the front blade to push small rocks. Use the arm to pick up the smallest rock, then turn and place it off the route. Use the rear tamper to mark a compaction pass. Large rocks block the rover. Autonomous avoidance uses simulated range readings and aims for the far end of the course. Obstacles are placed for this exercise. Contact uses a simplified geometric model.

The rover drives to the 21 m flag, then plans a return to the selected starting area. If blocked, it pauses and holds position.
