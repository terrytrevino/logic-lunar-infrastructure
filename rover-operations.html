Drive the rover around rocks

Open this course to see the rocks in the cameras above. Playing the mission trace switches the cameras to replay.

Use the buttons or arrow keys with focus on a driving control. Lower the front blade to push small rocks. Use the arm to pick up the smallest rock, then turn and place it off the route. Use the rear tamper to mark a compaction pass. Large rocks block the rover. Obstacles are placed for this exercise. Contact uses a simplified geometric model.
